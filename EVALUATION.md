# Behavioural Evaluation

Use these cases in a fresh Codex task or with an independent model. Load the skill normally and judge the result using the rubric below. Do not give the model the expected answer.

## Case 1 EAL full response

```text
Use $vcaa-text-response-style in EAL mode to write an 850-950 word response:
“To what extent is Sunset Boulevard about the loss of control?”
Use general, well-known evidence.
```

The response should answer the degree of agreement, organise paragraphs by concepts, use central scenes from different parts of the film, and remain clear without sounding like a university article.

## Case 2 English plan

```text
Use $vcaa-text-response-style in English mode to produce a contention and
three-paragraph plan for: “In Sunset Boulevard, Wilder suggests that
individuals can be both victims and villains. Discuss.”
```

The plan should address both sides of the relationship in each relevant argument. A plan that discusses villainy alone has misread the topic.

## Case 3 Revision without voice replacement

```text
Use $vcaa-text-response-style to revise the paragraph below. Preserve the
student's vocabulary and sentence range, remove generic AI language, and make
the analysis more precise. Explain no changes; return only the revision.

[Insert a real student paragraph.]
```

The revision should improve topic control and evidence-to-meaning reasoning without turning the student's voice into dense academic prose.

## Case 4 Evidence uncertainty

```text
Use $vcaa-text-response-style to write one body paragraph about Hollywood's
infatuation with youth. If any quotation or camera detail is uncertain,
paraphrase it rather than inventing it.
```

The paragraph should rely on recognisable scenes and proportionate claims. It should not build a major interpretation on an obscure prop, reflection or unverified shot.

## Review rubric

Score each category from 0 to 2.

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Topic control | Replaces or ignores part of the topic | Addresses the main idea but misses a modifier or relationship | Answers every concept, modifier and command word |
| Argument design | Plot list or one character per paragraph | Relevant arguments with limited progression | Distinct concept-led arguments that build or qualify the contention |
| Evidence | Invented, obscure or concentrated in one scene | Mostly accurate but unevenly selected | Central, verifiable evidence distributed across the film |
| Construction analysis | Technique labels or plot proof | Some connection between choice and meaning | Specific detail leads through authorial choice to meaning and topic |
| Voice | Generic AI prose or implausible academic density | Generally clear with occasional inflated phrasing | Natural, direct and plausible for the requested VCE register |
| Accuracy | Fabricated quotations or film details | Minor uncertainty that does not drive the claim | Quotations and details are verified or safely paraphrased |

A strong result scores at least 10 out of 12 and receives no zero in topic control or accuracy.
